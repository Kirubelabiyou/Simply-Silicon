How every workload runs.

Two things make a workload private. The hardware it runs on and the network that reaches it.

On bare metal

A physical GPU server dedicated to one customer, with no shared virtual layer in between. You choose the models and the software.

Over private fiber

A direct air-gapped line from your location to our site. Your data travels on our network and never on the public one.

What every workload gets.

Power and cooling run n+1 redundant. Private fiber stays air-gapped from the public network. Latency is backed by reliability SLAs. Routing stays local and deterministic. Public internet access is n+1 when you need it. Compute is high-density GPU on dedicated bare metal.
