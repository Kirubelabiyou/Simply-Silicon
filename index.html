Secure, localized compute at scale.

Simply Silicon turns latent utility infrastructure and own-use power into bare metal inference compute faster than conventional developers. Our long term goal is to connect these sites into a second internet for superintelligent models so they don't break the internet humans need.

How it works ↓

The internet was not built for inference.

The internet moves information that already exists. Inference produces new intelligence, one token at a time. They are different physical problems, and they need different infrastructure.

TopicThe internetInference
Built toDistribute information between humansProduce intelligence between machines
Unit of workThe webpage, the video segment, the API responseThe token
RewardsReach, redundancy, and scaleMemory locality, bandwidth density, low-latency interconnect, and thermal control
DistanceTolerated as the price of all threePunished at every hop
Designed forHumans clicking, reading, watching, downloading, and waitingPersistent machine-speed cognitive recursion

“Inference is punished by every extra hop because every hop increases the physical cost of maintaining state.”

The Internet Was Not Built for Inference

Traditional data centres and networks were not built for inference, let alone superintelligent models too capable for the public internet. We are rethinking the physical data centre and its networking from the ground up.

How it works.

Simply Silicon integrates low lead time urban power capacity, dense connectivity, and high performance inference racks into one physical platform.

01

Power

Low lead time urban power capacity.

Simply Silicon builds on latent utility infrastructure and own-use power that already exists in the city.

At our first site: dual redundant grid feeds, on-site generation, and 14 MVA n+1.

02

Connectivity

Dense connectivity on private, air-gapped fiber.

Private fiber physically eliminates the security threats posed by common carrier networks, while latency reliability SLAs support applications where delayed inference is a service failure.

Private circuits remove common carrier exposure for high-security workloads and superintelligent models.

03

Compute

High performance inference racks.

High-density GPU compute for bare metal or API applications, inside the city it serves.

Highly redundant infrastructure coupled with deterministic local routing supports latency-sensitive workloads and critical AI services.

Built for critical demand.

A physically-secure inference network for applications that cannot compromise.
