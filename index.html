    What we do

    Three moves. That's the whole idea.

    We are rethinking the physical data centre and its networking from the ground up. In plain terms, it comes down to this.

    01 · Power

    Find power that's already in the city.

    Utility sites like heating plants already have big grid connections and backup generation. We use that instead of waiting years to build new.

    02 · Compute

    Fill it with GPU servers.

    We install bare metal servers built for inference, the part of AI that answers questions in real time. No shared cloud layer on top.

    03 · Network

    Link sites on private fiber.

    Private fiber, kept physically apart from the public internet, connects our sites and the buildings around them. That keeps it fast and secure.

    Who it's for

    For AI that can't sit on the public internet.

    Physical separation for safety and latency sensitive AI. These are the customers who need it most.

    Banks · Utilities · Energy

    Regulated enterprise

    Companies with strict rules on where their data lives. Their AI runs in a known building, in their city, on their own servers.

    Physically separate

    High-security workloads

    Work that should never touch shared networks. An air-gapped network means there is no path in from the public internet.

    Always on

    Critical AI services

    AI that people depend on, backed by n+1 power and on-site generation so a single failure doesn't take it down.

    Low latency

    Live real-time AI

    Answers that need to arrive instantly. The servers are downtown, a few kilometres of private fiber from the users.
