Put together

One request, start to finish.

Here is what happens when a bank downtown asks its AI a question on Simply Silicon.

01

The question

An app in an office tower sends a request to its AI model.

02

Private fiber

It travels a few kilometres under the street on our own fiber. No public internet.

03

Air-gapped network

It enters a network with no connection to the outside world.

04

GPU server

A bare metal GPU server at Foundation runs the model, on n+1 power from the plant.

05

The answer

The answer takes the same short, private path back. Fast and contained.

The bigger picture

A second internet for AI.

The long-term goal is to connect these city sites into their own network for the most capable AI models, so they don't break the internet humans need.
