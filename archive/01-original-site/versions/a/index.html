What's different from a public cloud

On a public AI cloud you share servers with other customers and your data crosses the public internet. Here the hardware is used by you alone, and so is the line that reaches it.

Two services: the servers, and the line to them

Dedicated GPU servers

Your own bare-metal servers in our data centre. There's no shared virtual layer: you choose the models and the software, and the tokens you generate are yours.

Private fiber

A direct line from your building to ours, separate from the public internet and from anyone else's traffic. Your data never crosses the public network.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to run their own models on hardware only they use. That includes:

Banks
Use AI on client data without a public cloud.
Healthcare
Keep patient data private while your teams use current models.
Government
Run sensitive work on hardware you control.
Software companies
Serve open source models in your product from dedicated hardware.

How you start

From the first call to running your own models.

  1. Tell us the workload. What you need to run, and how much of it.
  2. We set up your servers. Dedicated GPUs, configured for you.
  3. You connect over private fiber from your own location.
  4. You run any model you like. Your data and your tokens stay with you.
