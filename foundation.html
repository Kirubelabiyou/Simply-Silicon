Inside a district heating plant.

We are rethinking the physical data centre and its networking from the ground up. Foundation does not start from an empty field. It starts inside a plant that already heats downtown buildings through underground pipes.

That plant brings what a new data centre waits years for: large power connections, redundant grid feeds and on-site generation. It also gives the heat from our GPUs somewhere useful to go. Liquid-cooled racks feed a heat exchanger tied to the plant, and the heat moves into the district loop instead of the air.

From the plant, private fiber runs under the streets to more than 25 connected buildings, physically separate from the public internet.
