About the site.

Location

Foundation sits at 435 9 Ave SE in downtown Calgary, inside an existing district heating plant.

The plant

The plant gives the site utility-grade infrastructure from day one. Below-grade thermal conduit paths and the district heating loop were already in place before the first rack arrived.

Power

Dual redundant grid feeds and on-site generation supply 14 MVA n+1. At full build the site delivers 12 MW of n+1 redundant power and cooling.

Network

A 7 km air-gapped fiber network links the site to more than 25 buildings downtown. n+1 public internet connectivity is there when a workload needs it.

Compute

High-density GPU compute is offered as dedicated bare metal, so every server belongs to one customer.
