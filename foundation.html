Flagship data centre.

Foundation integrates 12 MW of n+1 redundant power and cooling, a 7 km air-gapped fiber network with 25+ connected buildings, n+1 public internet connectivity, and high-density GPU compute as dedicated bare metal.

Foundation is particularly well-suited to serve superintelligent models that require high assurance containment to keep them off the public internet.

We are rethinking the physical data centre and its networking from the ground up.
