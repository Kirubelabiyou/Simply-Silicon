Who runs on it

Teams that want their own models on hardware only they use.

  • Banks use AI on client data without a public cloud.
  • Healthcare teams keep patient data private while using current models.
  • Government runs sensitive work on hardware it controls.
  • Software companies serve open source models in their product from dedicated hardware.

Getting started

Four steps from the first call to running your own models.

  1. Tell us what you need to run, and how much.
  2. We set up dedicated GPU servers for you.
  3. You connect over air-gapped fiber from your location.
  4. You run any model. Your data and tokens stay with you.
