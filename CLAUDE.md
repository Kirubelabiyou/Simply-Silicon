# Working with Kiru on Simply Silicon

## How outputs should look (always)
- Clean, simple, understandable, direct, but **designed and attractive, never bland**.
  Think "made by a good designer in Canva", not plain black-and-white, and not AI slop
  (no glowing gradients, random circuit art, stock 3D renders or busy filler).
- Use bold color blocks, real photos (Pexels is fine, not only Simply Silicon photos),
  simple flat icon figures, charts and diagrams where they explain something.
- One idea per page or slide. Plenty of space. Big, confident type.
- Write like a person talks: short sentences, plain words, no jargon or hype.
- Videos need a voiceover and background music.

## Visual kit (used for the 90-day plan event materials)
- Colors: navy #0F1A3C, electric blue #2F5BFF, orange #FF7A2F, lime #D7F75B, cream #FFF6EA.
- Fonts: Archivo Black (headings), Inter (text). Files in `plan-90day/fonts/`.
- Rounded photo cards, pill chips, a rotated "sticker" circle for dates or calls to action.

## Context
- Simply Silicon: secure, localized AI compute. First site is Foundation, 435 9 Ave SE, downtown Calgary
  (inside a district heating plant; 12 MW at full build, 100 kW online, 2 MW Phase 2 Feb 2027, 7 km private fiber, 25+ buildings).
- Kiru's 90-day plan starts Jan 2, 2027. Two tracks:
  1. Public understanding of data centers (events, video and written content, conversations).
  2. A repeatable workflow/system for how a site runs (operations, finances, contracts).
- 90-day plan files live in `plan-90day/`.
